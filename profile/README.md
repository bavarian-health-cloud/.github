# Bavarian Health Cloud (BHC) – Technical Public Identity

## About Bavarian Health Cloud

**Bavarian Health Cloud** is an innovative, publicly-owned data platform for health research in Bavaria. Owned 100% by the Free State of Bavaria, BHC serves as the technical infrastructure for aggregating, standardizing, and providing secure access to pseudonymized health data across Bavaria's research ecosystem.

## Mission

To **unlock the potential of Bavarian health data** by bundling and linking health data from medical institutions to make it easily accessible for researchers, advancing medical research that serves the common good while establishing Bavaria as an innovation hub for health research.

## Key Capabilities

### 📊 Integrated Health Data
- Combines pseudonymized data from all six Bavarian university hospitals
- Plans to integrate additional sources from outpatient and inpatient care
- Data is standardized, quality-controlled, and enables cross-location research

### 🔒 Security & Scalability
- Cloud-based platform providing flexible storage and compute capacity
- Secure research environment with exclusive access controls
- Enterprise-grade data protection and compliance

### ⚡ Research Efficiency
- Metadata catalog allows researchers to preview available data
- Streamlined approval process for quick access without unnecessary bureaucracy
- Transparent, efficient access to research-grade health data

### 🔗 Standards & Interoperability
- Built on established health data standards (FHIR R4, HL7, others)
- Designed for interoperability with national initiatives
- Integration roadmap aligned with European Health Data Space (EHDS)

## Current Projects

### [FHIRScan](https://github.com/bavarian-health-cloud/FHIRScan)
A comprehensive Python tool for analyzing large-scale FHIR R4 datasets to understand structure, completeness, and value distributions — without requiring full data flattening. Supports both file-based and live FHIR server profiling.

## Partners & Collaborators

BHC collaborates with:
